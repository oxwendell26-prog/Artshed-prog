{# How Escrow Works Guide #}

How Escrow Purchase Works

  1. Reserve Item: Placing your order reserves the artwork immediately.
  2. Upload Payment: You upload your proof of payment to the official platform Escrow account.
  3. Admin Verification: Admin manually checks the platform account and receipt, then records verified funds in the escrow ledger before the artist prepares the painting.
  4. Receive & Release: After you inspect and confirm receipt, the net payout (price less 3%) is recorded. Admin then transfers it to the artist and marks it released.
